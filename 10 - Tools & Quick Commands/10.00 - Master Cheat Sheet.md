## Exam Workflow

1. Enumerate broadly, then enumerate each service deeply.
2. Record every credential, hash, host, port, share, user, group, and trust as you find it.
3. Reuse every credential against every relevant service and host.
4. After each foothold, enumerate locally before pivoting further.
5. Re-run enumeration after any privilege change or new credential.
6. Capture exact commands and evidence as you go.

Full version: [[10.02 - Quick Reference#Exam Workflow Reminder]]
