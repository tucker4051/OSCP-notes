## Active Directory Attack Comparison

|Technique|Required Access|Prerequisite|Result|Offline Cracking|Typical Use|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Password Spray|None / external access may suffice|Valid username list|Valid plaintext credential|No|Initial access|
|AS-REP Roasting|Domain user or known usernames|Account with Kerberos pre-authentication disabled|AS-REP hash|Yes|Compromise a vulnerable account|
|Kerberoasting|Domain user|SPN-linked service account|TGS hash|Yes|Compromise a service account|
|Silver Ticket|Service-account key/hash|Service account material and domain details|Forged service ticket|No|Access a specific service|
|DCSync|Replication rights or equivalent privilege|High-privilege domain access|Domain credential hashes|No|Credential extraction|
|Pass the Hash|NTLM hash|Reachable NTLM-compatible service|Authenticated session|No|Immediate authentication|
|Overpass the Hash|NTLM hash|Kerberos-capable domain context|Kerberos TGT|No|Kerberos-based access|
|Pass the Ticket|Ticket access|Valid active Kerberos ticket|Reused Kerberos session|No|Ticket reuse|
|Golden Ticket|`krbtgt` key/hash|Domain SID and `krbtgt` material|Forged TGT|No|Domain-wide persistence/access|

## Exam Workflow Reminder

1. Enumerate broadly, then enumerate each discovered service deeply.
    
2. Record credentials, hashes, hosts, ports, shares, users, groups, and trust relationships immediately.
    
3. Reuse every credential against every relevant service and host.
    
4. After each foothold, perform local enumeration before pivoting.
    
5. Re-run enumeration after privilege changes or obtaining new credentials.
    
6. Capture exact commands and evidence as you work.
